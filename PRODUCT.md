# SWEED Product Context

SWEED is an Arabic-first agency website for marketing, advertising, creative production, offers, products, portfolio, articles, FAQs, and contact conversion.

## Users

- Egyptian business owners.
- Marketing managers.
- Founders comparing agency options.
- Leads who prefer WhatsApp over long forms.

## Jobs To Be Done

- Understand what SWEED does quickly.
- Trust the agency from visual quality, copy, portfolio, and offers.
- Pick a service or offer.
- Contact SWEED with minimal friction.

## Brand Voice

- Direct.
- Confident.
- Practical.
- Arabic-first.
- No overhyped AI-style copy.

## Design Constraints

- Use SWEED pink `#ed2062` and deep purple `#261b3e`.
- Keep typography readable in Arabic.
- Preserve existing route structure unless explicitly approved.
- Prefer modular React sections under `apps/web/src`.
- Do not expand legacy HTML except for urgent temporary fixes.
