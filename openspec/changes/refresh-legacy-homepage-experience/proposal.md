# Proposal

## Goal

Refresh the accepted SWEED legacy homepage without replacing the legacy structure or redesigning the whole site.

## Scope

- Sticky top bar and header behavior.
- Official logo usage.
- Hero gradient, white consultation button, and rotating business backgrounds.
- Mobile social side rail.
- Compact problems, clients, portfolio, and services sections.
- Quick consultation CTA routing into the contact page.
- Contact service field changed from single select to multi-select.

## Non-goals

- Rebuilding the homepage in React components.
- Replacing the accepted legacy page source.
- Changing all internal pages beyond the contact form field required for routing.
