## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Public marketing pages
The system SHALL provide polished public pages for SWEED's brand, services, offers/packages, products, portfolio, articles/news, FAQ, and contact.

#### Scenario: Visitor lands on home page
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the home page
- **THEN** the page MUST communicate SWEED's offer, key services, proof, package path, and contact action without needing another page first

#### Scenario: Visitor explores services
- **WHEN** a visitor opens services
- **THEN** the page MUST show service categories, benefits, process, AI demo entry, and links to relevant details or contact actions

### Requirement: Conversion-oriented navigation
The system SHALL provide clear desktop and mobile navigation with visible routes and primary contact actions.

#### Scenario: Mobile visitor opens navigation
- **WHEN** a mobile visitor opens the menu
- **THEN** all primary routes and contact actions MUST be reachable without layout overlap

#### Scenario: Visitor wants to contact SWEED
- **WHEN** a visitor decides to contact SWEED from any major page
- **THEN** a clear call-to-action MUST route them to contact or package guidance

### Requirement: Responsive visual quality
The system SHALL render professionally across mobile, tablet, and desktop without text overflow, incoherent overlap, or broken RTL/LTR mixed content.

#### Scenario: Narrow mobile viewport
- **WHEN** the website is viewed at a narrow mobile width
- **THEN** text, navigation, cards, buttons, and interactive surfaces MUST remain readable and usable

### Requirement: Article and news experience
The system SHALL include article listing and article detail experiences suitable for future CMS-backed news or insights.

#### Scenario: Visitor opens article list
- **WHEN** a visitor opens articles/news
- **THEN** the system MUST show article summaries, categories or tags, dates, and links to detail pages

#### Scenario: Visitor opens article detail
- **WHEN** a visitor opens an article detail page
- **THEN** the system MUST show article title, metadata, body content, related navigation, and SEO metadata
