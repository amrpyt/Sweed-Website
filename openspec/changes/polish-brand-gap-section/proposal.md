# Polish Brand Gap Section

## Summary

Fix the homepage section currently labeled `SWEED / BRAND PRESENCE` with the headline `We close / That gap` so it feels intentional, Arabic-first, and aligned with SWEED's dark brand color.

## Problems

- The section uses a generic near-black background instead of the committed SWEED dark purple.
- The English headline interrupts the otherwise Arabic homepage experience.
- The split headline and image stack can feel visually disconnected rather than communicating one clear brand-presence message.
- The micro-grid, muted gray copy, and tiny uppercase eyebrow feel more like a template treatment than a SWEED section.

## Scope

- Keep the existing scroll/pin/image-reveal interaction.
- Replace the English label/headline/copy with sharper Arabic-first content.
- Use SWEED's dark purple `#261b3e` as the section surface and `#ed2062` as the accent.
- Improve contrast, spacing, and responsive layout without touching legacy static HTML.
- Add regression coverage for the section content and brand-color surface.
- Build, deploy to the demo service, review in browser, and commit.
